# Physical and numerical assumptions

All new trial outputs are **synthetic demonstrations**. The declared polymer index 1.52+0i, substrate 1.45+0i and ambient 1+0i are assumed generic dielectrics at 980 nm. They are not measured IP-Dip, calibrated silica or cured/uncured resin data. An attempted external ellipsometry-paper fetch timed out; no values were silently taken from an unread source. Actual resin data remain an unmet requirement for a physical reproduction.

The supplied Hadibrata main PDF and publisher SI were read and checked against rendered pages. Benchmark A (980 nm characterization) and B (780 nm writing) remain separate in `reference.yaml`. A 220 nm printed feature is not an optical spot width. The simulated 76% and measured 73% collection definitions differ and are not our fixed-radius efficiency. Exact CAD is unavailable: 32 uniform annuli are a restricted same-specification design. The paper's 1.7 scaling factor is not used.

All lengths are micrometres, vacuum wavelengths are micrometres, heights are measured from the substrate surface at z=0, and focal distances are measured from the fixed end of the design domain at z=2.5. The substrate is homogeneous below z=0. Each nonzero polymer annulus contacts it. There is no buffer in the 980 nm trial. An explicit 0.1 um thick PEC aperture screen occupies r>12.4 and -0.1<z<0 in Maxwell; the proxy uses the same clear aperture. This stop is an assumed experimental modification, not supplied paper CAD. Incident illumination is an x-polarized normal plane wave from the substrate. Supports and aperture are common to all baselines.

The nominal end-plane geometric NA is n*r/sqrt(r^2+f^2). The substrate-plane pupil has a different geometric distance, f+2.5; both are reported rather than independently targeting NA=0.85. The proxy treats the finite-height lens as a thin local transmission element at the exit reference plane. This approximation can shift focus relative to Maxwell and must be checked, especially at high NA.

The local transmission is the normal-incidence three-medium thin-film amplitude, including its normal-incidence multiple reflections and the remaining ambient optical path. Angular-spectrum propagation uses vector transverse projection, longitudinal electric fields, magnetic fields and Poynting flux in the declared ambient. Evanescent content and directions above NA/n=0.98 are discarded. Total vector-spectrum power is normalized to the local thin-film transmitted power. Internal lateral scattering, aperture-edge near fields, ring coupling and oblique interface effects are not resolved by this proxy.

The 0.3 um minimum width/gap, 0.05 um height grid and aspect ratio 8 are assumed trial fabrication constraints. Optimization differentiates continuous physical heights with an explicit height-grid penalty. Evaluation projects to the height grid; all Maxwell inputs are projected supported binary geometry. Finite-difference checks concern the continuous proxy, not the discontinuous projection operation.

The fixed radius R=0.55 um, loss weights, axial range, train/validation/test targets and seeds are frozen in `configs/desktop_trial.json` before training. Test targets, including 7.8 um, never select network weights or feedback corrections. Direct optimization uses the same material/geometry/loss and records a matched aggregate objective-forward budget. Full axial scans and final reporting are recorded separately from optimization calls. A trained Fresnel-correction network is not evidence of superiority over a Fresnel lens.

Meep runs are independent CPU subprocesses. The m=+1 source uses Er and +i Ep; reflection supplies the equivalent m=-1 sector. Equal half-amplitude sectors reconstruct x-linear illumination: Er=a_r cos(phi), Ep=i*a_p sin(phi), Ez=a_z cos(phi); Hr=i*b_r sin(phi), Hp=b_p cos(phi), Hz=i*b_z sin(phi). Azimuthally integrated linear power is half the full m=+1 circular-sector power. The same factor cancels in all power ratios. No m=0 scalar substitution or Meep-to-JAX backpropagation is used. Mesh, boundary, PML and duration checks must pass before any Maxwell result is called converged.

Meep's requested top boundary at z=14 um would intersect the focal scan ending at z=14.5 um. The executable clearance rule uses top=max(requested_top, exit_plane+scan_max+PML+2.5 um), and a transmission cap 1 um inside the inner PML boundary. Standard effective top is 18.5 um, transmission cap 16 um, bottom -3.5 um; all requested/effective positions are recorded. The spacing test increases radial padding by 1.5 um. PML thickness and duration are tested independently. Power closure uses incident/reflected flux at z=-0.5, the finite top cap, and the cylindrical side surface. Proxy full-plane transmission and Meep finite-cap transmission are different reported observables; fixed-radius target-plane efficiency has the common definition.

Cylindrical point DFT monitors at r=0 returned alternating zeros at half-grid axial positions at resolution 30, while the nonempty radial field slices remained smooth. Focus extraction therefore uses |Er|^2+|Ez|^2 from the first centered radial Yee voxel, within one radial cell of the axis. The raw point data are retained. Width coordinates follow the official finite radial-slice convention and carry a one-cell uncertainty. Widths use the nearest 0.25 um axial scan plane to each design's maximum. No geometry or power-flux data were altered to repair field extraction.

The removed Windows free-RAM gate remains disabled for all desktop phases. Project RSS, WSL memory, GPU budgets, timeouts and compact storage remain monitored. Numerical smoke success and synthetic Maxwell success do not establish experimental polymer or printing performance.
